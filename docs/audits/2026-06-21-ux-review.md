## Summary

Prepio doesn't feel hard because it's missing features. It feels hard because a clean,
linear job — research a company, review the plan, practice, see where you're weak — is
presented as a flat menu of overlapping screens. The single highest-leverage move is to
reorganise the app around **one object: an "interview."** Everything — research, plan,
practice, review — lives inside it.

- **Scope:** Navigation & IA · first-run · prep plan · practice · visual system.
- **Method:** Code & flow walkthrough against Prepio's own stated design principles.
- **Output:** Diagnosis · proposed IA · wireframes · hi-fi mockups · roadmap.

## The five findings (ranked by impact on a first-time, time-pressured candidate)

1. **A linear journey is shown as a flat tab bar** — *Critical.* `Research → Plan →
   Practice → Review` is one path, but the nav lists six equal destinations
   (`src/components/Navigation.tsx:60-67`). Users never feel where they are or what's next.
2. **Three different ways to pick a research run** — *Critical.* An "Active research"
   dropdown (`Navigation.tsx:202-226`), a left "History" sheet (`228-311`), and a separate
   `/history` page all switch/recall runs. Hidden state means the Dashboard can silently
   show empty.
3. **The prep plan is six dense panels, much duplicated** — *High.* Summary, priority
   strip, fake-AI "Quick guidance", stage roadmap, leverage questions, and a deep-dive
   (`src/pages/Dashboard.tsx`). Priorities and questions each appear twice; the one real
   action (Start practice) gets lost.
4. **New users hit empty screens that explain the menu** — *High.* `Dashboard.tsx:1032/1044`
   reads "No Active Search → Open the History menu in the top bar." That's the UI explaining
   its own mechanics — exactly what `DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md` says to avoid.
5. **A busy, inconsistent surface adds friction** — *Polish.* Four corner-radius sizes,
   uppercase micro-labels everywhere, five badge colours, and cards-inside-cards.

> **If you do one thing:** reorganise around one "interview." That single change dissolves
> findings 1, 2, and 4 at once.

## What the product should feel like — four principles

Measured against Prepio's own strategy/design docs:

1. **One obvious next action** — every screen answers "what do I do now?" with a single
   primary button.
2. **A journey, not a menu** — position in the research → readiness arc is always visible.
3. **Calm by default** — show the essential; tuck analytical depth behind progressive
   disclosure.
4. **Honest, specific copy** — no fake AI chat, no vague scores.

## Systematic audit (five areas, worst-first)

- **A · Navigation & information architecture** *(Critical)* — six peer tabs flatten a
  four-step journey; three overlapping "which run" controls; "Dashboard" is mislabeled and
  its state is an invisible `?searchId`.
- **B · First run & empty states** *(High)* — empty states explain the menu instead of
  acting; no guided "set up your first interview".
- **C · The prep plan** *(High)* — six stacked panels with duplication; the "Quick
  guidance" panel reads like an AI demo; the primary action competes instead of dominating;
  stage selection is a config step before any value.
- **D · The practice loop** *(High)* — getting *into* practice takes four gates; progress &
  "what's weak" live on a separate page instead of pulling users back.
- **E · Visual system** *(Polish)* — four radii, uppercase labels everywhere, badge & box
  overload.

## Target information architecture

Six tabs collapse into **one home + one workspace**:

- **Home — "Your interviews":** each saved run is a card with its own status, progress, and
  one-tap way back in. Replaces the Dashboard empty state, the `/history` page, *and* the
  "Active research" dropdown.
- **One interview = one workspace:** a persistent identity header ("Stripe · Senior PM")
  over three segments — **Plan / Practice / Review**. No hidden `?searchId`.
- **Account menu:** Profile, Billing/Pricing, Sign out step out of the primary nav.

Headline deltas: **6 → 2** primary destinations · **3 → 1** ways to switch interview ·
**0** empty screens that explain the menu.

## Roadmap (sequenced by impact-per-hour)

Most of this is reorganising screens that already exist, not a rewrite. Ship P0 first.

### P0 — Fix the structure (~1 weekend; removes ~60% of the friction)

- **P0-01** Build the "Your interviews" home; make it the post-login landing.
- **P0-02** Collapse nav to Interviews + account menu; add the Plan / Practice / Review
  header; rename Dashboard → Plan.
- **P0-03** Delete the redundant research switchers (Active-research dropdown + History sheet).

### P1 — Calm the Plan & ease into Practice (~few days)

- **P1-04** Cut the Plan to hero + one CTA + roadmap + collapsed "Why this plan".
- **P1-05** Replace "Quick guidance" with a "Start here" line on the top stage.
- **P1-06** One-tap practice from cards; default to all stages.

### P2 — Polish the surface & close the loop (ongoing)

- **P2-07** Lock a design system: 2 radii, 1 accent, ≤2 badge styles, fewer caps labels.
- **P2-08** Surface "needs work" & progress on the interview card and Review tab.
